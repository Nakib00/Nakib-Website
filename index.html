Hi, I'm Md Nakibul Islam.

I'm a software engineer and founder based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My co-authored research spans IoT sensing and Bangla speech datasets, published in IEEE and on Mendeley Data. Let's connect to explore opportunities in tech, robotics education, and sustainability.
